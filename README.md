### Project Idea
Have you ever watched someone at the table say "no thanks" to dessert because it just wasn't safe for them? Krimoo was born from that moment.

We make delicious frozen desserts for everyone, no matter their allergies, health conditions, or fitness goals. Our idea is simple: **one freezer, the whole family, and nobody left out.**

---

## What Makes Krimoo Different
* **Fair prices for everyone:** Every product costs just 50 EGP. Imported specialty pints usually cost 200+ EGP, so we wanted a high-quality local option people can actually afford.
* **A range built around real needs, not a one-size-fits-all compromise:**
  * **GlycoSmart:** Low-glycemic ice cream with no refined sugar. We sweeten it naturally with monk fruit and stevia, so blood sugar stays calm.
  * **PureCare:** Gluten-free, dairy-free, and made from oat and coconut, for people with celiac disease or lactose intolerance. Always made nut-free.
  * **ProFit:** High-protein, lower-calorie ice cream with 20g+ of protein per pint, made for active lifestyles.
* **Safety you can trust:** Clear labels on the front of every pack, separate production lines, and strict anti-contamination routines, so allergy sufferers can relax and enjoy.
* **Real ice cream taste:** Our oat-and-coconut base is creamy and scoopable, with no "diet food" aftertaste.
* **Flavors you grew up loving:** Pistachio Knafeh, Mastic, Karkadeh, Oreo, and rich Chocolate, reimagined in a healthier way.
* **Easy to find:** Order from our web store, get it in 30 minutes through quick delivery, or grab it at mall pop-ups and in the health aisles of specialty supermarkets.

---

## Who We Made It For
* **People managing diabetes or following keto:** Safe, low-glycemic treats that won't spike blood sugar (`GlycoSmart`).
* **People with celiac disease, lactose intolerance, or nut and dairy allergies:** Finally, certified-safe desserts (`PureCare`).
* **Gym-goers and athletes:** A clean, high-protein post-workout treat (`ProFit`).
* **Parents and families (ages 28 to 48):** Wholesome desserts the whole family can share ("One Freezer for All").
* **Gen Z and young professionals in Greater Cairo:** Health-conscious, trend-aware people who love mall pop-ups and ordering online.
